About ZEVUNOR

ZEVUNOR is a modern platform for creating personalized products, founded in 2020 in Barcelona, Spain.

We use advanced printing technologies and only certified materials to ensure the highest quality of every item.

Why choose us

  • Own production — full quality control at every stage
  • Fast delivery — across Spain in 2-3 days
  • Quality guarantee — we remake for free or refund
  • Eco-friendly — biodegradable packaging
  • 10,000+ clients in 35 countries

Our mission

We believe every gift should be unique. Our goal is to help you create memorable moments that last forever.

Have questions? Write to us at info@zevunor.com or via WhatsApp — we reply within 1 hour.

Home › Returns

Returns & warranty

✅ 100% satisfaction guarantee If you're not happy with the item — we'll remake it for free or refund within 30 days.

Right of return (14 days)

According to EU Directive 2011/83/EU, you have the right to return any product within 14 days of receipt, without giving a reason.

Return procedure

  1. Contact us via WhatsApp or email
  2. Get return instructions and address
  3. Send the item back in original packaging
  4. Refund within 14 days after we receive it

Exceptions

Personalized items made to your specifications can't be returned unless defective.
